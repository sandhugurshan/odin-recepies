Jalebi



DESCRIPTION

Jalebi is a classic Indian sweet made by deep-frying a fermented batter of all-purpose flour (maida), cornstarch, and yogurt into intricate, coiled spirals. The crisp, golden-fried swirls are immediately immersed in a warm, fragrant sugar syrup infused with saffron and cardamom, which they absorb greedily to become beautifully translucent. Each bite offers a delightful contrast of textures, delivering a crunch that gives way to a burst of sweet, tangy syrup. Traditionally served hot, these glossy, coiled treats are a staple celebration dessert, often paired with creamy rabdi or savory fafda to balance the sweetness.

INGREDIENTS

  • All-purpose flour (maida): 1 cup
  • • Cornflour or gram flour (besan): 2 tablespoons (for extra crispiness)
  • Plain yogurt (curd): 1 to 4 tablespoons (provides the classic slight tang)
  • Water: 1/2 to 3/4 cup (adjusted to get a smooth, flowing consistency)
  • Baking soda or baking powder: 1/4 teaspoon

Steps in making Jalebi

  1. Prepare the Sugar Syrup
    • • Combine sugar and water in a pan over medium heat.
    • Add cardamom powder and saffron strands.
    • Boil until the syrup reaches a single-string consistency (sticky between your fingers).
    • Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice, then keep the syrup warm
  2. Make the Jalebi Batter
    • Whisk together all-purpose flour, chickpea flour, yogurt, baking powder, baking soda, and water until smooth and lump-free.
    • The batter should have a flowing, ribbon-like consistency (similar to pancake batter).
    • Let it rest briefly or ferment for a slight tang.
  3. Pipe and Fry
    • Transfer the batter into a squeeze bottle or a piping bag with a small (2–3 mm) opening.
    • Heat oil or ghee in a wide, flat pan over medium heat.
    • Squeeze the batter in concentric circles starting from the inside out to form spirals.
    • Fry on medium heat until both sides turn crisp and light golden.
  4. Soak in Syrup
    • Remove the fried jalebis from the oil using tongs or a slotted spoon, letting excess oil drip off.
    • Immediately submerge them in the warm sugar syrup for about 30 to 60 seconds so they absorb the liquid.
    • Take them out and serve warm.

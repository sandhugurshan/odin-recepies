SAMOSA



DESCRIPTION

A samosa is a popular fried or baked pastry with a savory filling, usually made in a triangular shape.

INGREDIENTS

  • • All-purpose flour (Maida): 2 cups (approx. 250–260 grams)
  • Carom seeds (Ajwain): 1 teaspoon (crushed for flavor and digestion)
  • Salt: ¼ to 1 teaspoon (to taste)
  • • Ghee or oil: ¼ cup (melted ghee or neutral oil for the flaky texture)
  • Water: About 5 to 6 tablespoons (added gradually to make a stiff dough)
  • • Potatoes: 4 medium-sized (500 grams), boiled, peeled, and mashed or diced
  • • Green peas: ½ cup (boiled or fresh/frozen soaked in warm water)

Steps in making Samosa

  1. Make the Dough
    • • Mix the all-purpose flour, ajwain, salt, and melted ghee in a bowl.
    • • Rub the ghee into the flour with your fingertips until it resembles breadcrumbs.
    • Add cold water gradually and knead into a firm, stiff dough.
    • Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. . Prepare the Filling
    • Heat oil or ghee in a pan and add cumin seeds, crushed coriander seeds, fennel seeds, and a pinch of hing.
    • • Stir in the chopped ginger and green chilies, sautéing for a few seconds.
    • Add the mashed boiled potatoes, green peas, ground spices (coriander powder, garam masala, amchur, red chili powder), and salt.
    • • Mix well while mashing the potatoes slightly, cook for 3 to 4 minutes, then stir in fresh coriander and let the filling cool completely.
  3. Shape and Assemble
    • Divide the rested dough into equal balls and roll each ball into an oval shape about 6 to 7 inches long
    • Cut the oval in half horizontally with a knife to create two semi-circles.
    • Rub a little water along the straight edge of a semi-circle.
    • Bring the two corners of the straight edge together, overlapping them slightly to form a cone.
    • Stuff the cone with 2 to 3 tablespoons of the cooled potato filling.
    • Moistener the top open edges with water, make a small pleat on the back edge, and pinch the sides firmly together to seal the samosa completely.
  4. Fry the Samosas
    • Heat frying oil in a deep pan over low-to-medium heat.
    • Slide the samosas gently into the oil, ensuring the oil is not too hot (it should barely bubble at first)
    • Fry the samosas slowly on low heat for 10 to 12 minutes, turning occasionally, until they turn crisp and golden brown.
    • Drain on paper towels and serve.
